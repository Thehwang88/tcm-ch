You can send your inquiry to TCM.ch in English. Before a visit is confirmed, ask the team to check that the **practitioner**, not just reception, can conduct the consultation in your preferred language.

## What to confirm before choosing

A useful consultation involves more than naming the painful area. You should be able to explain your medical history, understand the proposed treatment and alternatives, and say when something is uncomfortable. Ask about language availability for the whole visit.

Also check the practitioner's qualifications, the treatment method, current fees and relevant insurance recognition. A language preference does not replace those checks, and registration alone does not establish insurance cover.

## Find a convenient practice

Use the [regional clinic finder](/en/acupuncture-near-me/) to compare locations around home or work. Each [location page](/en/locations/) gives its current status, address and operator information.

In Zürich, distinguish the operating partner practice from requests for a new location: read the [Oerlikon page](/en/locations/zuerich-oerlikon/) carefully before selecting an option. You can also explore [Zürich Höngg](/en/locations/zuerich-hoengg/), [Basel](/en/locations/basel/) and [St. Gallen](/en/locations/st-gallen/). The location page is the source for current status; this article does not promise live slots.

## What to put in your inquiry

- Your preferred practice or the area where you live or work.
- English as the language you want for the consultation.
- A brief description of your main concern or treatment preference.
- Days and times that work for you.
- Any accessibility needs or practical questions.

Use the [English contact form](/en/contact/). The team will respond to arrange the details; submitting the form is an inquiry, not instant confirmation of an appointment.

## Insurance and costs

Ask your insurer about the named practitioner, method and policy. Do not rely on a statement that a whole clinic is recognised. CSS's guidance illustrates the need to check both provider and method for the relevant cover. [CSS benefits guidance](https://www.css.ch/en/private-customers/quick-easy/get-help/benefits-check/alternative-methods.html).

Our [insurance guide](/en/health-insurance-acupuncture/) and [cost checklist](/en/knowledge/how-much-does-acupuncture-cost-in-switzerland/) help you prepare these questions.

## Before your first visit

Bring a medication list, any relevant reports and a short account of what you have already tried. Ask how the practitioner will judge whether treatment is helping. Read [what to expect at the first appointment](/en/knowledge/what-to-expect-first-acupuncture-session/) for a walkthrough.
