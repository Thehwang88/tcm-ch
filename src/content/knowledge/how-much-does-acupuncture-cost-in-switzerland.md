The useful price is the fee for **your named practitioner and planned appointment**, not a national average. Clinics can differ in how they charge for the initial assessment, treatment time and additional services. Request a written estimate before booking.

## Ask for a complete quote

Use this checklist when contacting a practice:

| Ask about | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| First appointment | Does the fee include the consultation and treatment? |
| Follow-up visits | How long are they, and how is extra time charged? |
| Additional services | Are herbs, manual treatment or other products billed separately? |
| Cancellation | What notice is required and what fee applies? |
| Billing | When is payment due, and do you submit the invoice yourself? |

We do not publish a single network-wide fee here because the practice and treatment plan need to be confirmed. An inquiry is the right time to ask for the price, before agreeing to care.

## Estimate the cost of a course

Ask when progress will be reviewed rather than committing to an open-ended series. Your budget should include the first visit, any agreed follow-ups and separately charged products. Keep the number of appointments and the fee separate: neither is a promise of a particular result.

If an insurer contributes, your out-of-pocket cost is the invoice total minus the **actual approved reimbursement**. Do not assume a percentage without checking any applicable caps, exclusions or remaining allowance.

## Check insurance before treatment

Therapist-led acupuncture may qualify for supplementary cover. Appropriately qualified physician-led acupuncture follows different basic-insurance rules. Your insurer must confirm the practitioner, method and policy conditions. See the [insurance guide](/en/health-insurance-acupuncture/) for the distinction and official sources.

CSS, for example, tells policyholders to check recognised therapists and methods for their particular cover. That is one insurer's guidance, not a reimbursement promise for the whole network. [CSS benefits guidance](https://www.css.ch/en/private-customers/quick-easy/get-help/benefits-check/alternative-methods.html).

## If you are paying yourself

Ask for the same itemised quote and progress review. Tell the practitioner if your budget is limited so you can discuss realistic options before starting. You can ask questions or decide against treatment after hearing the proposed plan.
