Start by checking cover, not by assuming an invoice will be reimbursed. This guide is for an inquiry about therapist-led acupuncture; physician-led treatment can fall under different rules. The [insurance overview](/en/health-insurance-acupuncture/) explains the distinction.

## 1. Get confirmation before booking

Give your insurer the practitioner's full name and the proposed treatment method. Ask whether your policy covers that combination, what limits apply and whether a referral or advance approval is needed. Keep the reply or reference number.

If you are using an international policy, ask its administrator about treatment in Switzerland, provider requirements and required documents. Do not assume that a Swiss register entry meets a foreign policy's conditions.

## 2. Clarify how the practice bills

Ask whether you receive and pay the invoice yourself or whether an agreed direct-billing arrangement applies. Confirm the payment deadline separately from the insurer's processing time. A pending claim does not automatically change the practice's terms.

Keep the itemised invoice showing the patient, provider, dates, services and charges. Ask your insurer which provider identifiers or other details it needs; if something is missing, request a correction from the practice rather than editing the invoice yourself.

## 3. Use your insurer's submission route

Follow the instructions for the relevant policy and insurer. This may be an app, an online portal or another documented route. Helsana, for example, provides invoice-submission guidance through its customer information. [Helsana customer guidance](https://www.helsana.ch/en/individuals/services/welcome.html).

Keep a copy of the invoice and submission confirmation. Check the claim deadline and any request for additional documents. Processing times and reimbursement arrangements vary; we cannot promise when a claim will be paid.

## 4. Check the benefit statement

Compare the insurer's explanation with the invoice and your policy. Look at what was accepted, what was excluded and what allowance remains. A provider registration is only part of the coverage question; the treatment method and contract also matter. [CSS benefits guidance](https://www.css.ch/en/private-customers/quick-easy/get-help/benefits-check/alternative-methods.html).

## If the claim is refused

Ask for the specific reason in writing. It may involve policy cover, method or practitioner eligibility, a limit, or missing information. A factual invoice correction can help when information is wrong, but resubmitting cannot create cover that the policy excludes. Discuss unclear decisions with the insurer before booking further treatment on the assumption it will pay.
